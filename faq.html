Statutory redundancy pay FAQ

Common questions on statutory redundancy with statute cites. Verified 23 June 2026 against ERA 1996 and the 2026/27 rate Order.
Is statutory redundancy pay tax-free?
Yes, in full. Statutory redundancy pay is income-tax-free and NIC-free without any cap. The £30,000 tax-free threshold under ITEPA 2003 s.403 applies to the wider termination package (statutory plus enhanced top-up plus ex-gratia) but statutory alone never crosses it because the maximum statutory payment in 2026/27 is £22,530.
Do I get redundancy pay if I have worked less than 2 years?
No. The 2-year continuous service rule in ERA 1996 s.155 is absolute for statutory redundancy. There is no discretion. If you are dismissed at week 103, you get nothing. Some employers offer enhanced redundancy from day one or after 1 year; check your contract and staff handbook.
How is the weekly pay figure worked out if I have variable hours?
For variable-hours workers, a week's pay is the average of the last 52 paid weeks (skipping any unpaid weeks). For fixed-hours workers it is the contractual weekly gross. The figure is then capped at the statutory weekly limit, currently £751 from 6 April 2026.
Can my employer refuse to pay statutory redundancy?
Not lawfully. If you qualify and your employer refuses, file a complaint with an employment tribunal within 6 months of termination under ERA 1996 s.164. ACAS Early Conciliation is mandatory before issuing. If the employer is insolvent, claim from the National Insurance Fund via the Insolvency Service RP1 form.
Does PILON eat into my £30,000 tax-free allowance?
PILON itself is taxed as earnings and sits outside the £30k slice. But the Post-Employment Notice Pay (PENP) formula introduced in April 2018 carves a notice-equivalent slice out of any termination package and taxes it as earnings, regardless of whether the contract has a PILON clause. Only what remains after PENP is run against the £30k threshold.
Is the £30,000 tax-free threshold going to be uprated?
There is no current proposal to uprate the £30,000 threshold. It has been frozen at £30,000 since Finance Act 1988 with no indexation mechanism. Any change requires a fresh Finance Act provision. As of 23 June 2026 the Treasury has not published any proposal.
What is the maximum statutory redundancy payment for 2026/27?
£22,530. That is 20 years × 1.5 weeks per year × £751 weekly cap. The maximum applies only if you are age 41 or over for all 20 of your last 20 years, and your gross weekly pay is at or above £751. Most people receive much less.
Does the age-band formula discriminate against younger workers?
It does, but the discrimination is statutorily authorised. The Employment Equality (Age) Regulations 2006 reg.33 carved out an exemption for the statutory redundancy formula, on the policy ground that older workers face greater re-employment difficulty. The exemption now sits in Schedule 9 of the Equality Act 2010.
Do I get redundancy if I take voluntary redundancy?
Yes. Voluntary redundancy is treated as a dismissal for the same reason (redundancy) and qualifies for the same statutory minimum. Most voluntary offers come with an enhanced top-up because the employer wants to reduce the number of compulsory selections.
Does notice pay count toward my redundancy figure?
No. Statutory notice pay and statutory redundancy pay are separate. Notice pay is calculated as 1 week per year of service from year 2, capped at 12 weeks (ERA 1996 s.86) at your actual weekly gross (not the £751 cap). Notice pay is taxed as earnings; redundancy is tax-free.
What happens if my role is offered to me on lower pay or further away?
A meaningful drop in pay, status or significantly longer commute usually means the alternative is not 'suitable' under ERA 1996 s.141. You can refuse without losing statutory redundancy. The 4-week trial period under s.138 lets you try the role before deciding.
How long does it take to get paid?
Solvent employers must pay statutory redundancy on the termination date or shortly after, typically with the final payroll run. Insolvency Service payments via RP1 take 3 to 6 weeks from receipt of a complete claim and verification from the insolvency practitioner.
Does the rate apply on the date of the at-risk letter or the dismissal date?
The dismissal date. The cap is the one in force on the 'relevant date', which is the effective date of termination (last day worked or end of notice). A termination on 5 April 2026 uses £719; on 6 April 2026 or later, £751.
Can I claim backdated redundancy from a 2022 or earlier termination?
Generally no. The statutory claim window is 6 months from termination (ERA 1996 s.164) with a further 6 months tribunal discretion. After 12 months the door is usually shut. Insolvency Service claims via RP1 are more flexible if the insolvency was recent.
Does the formula differ for Scotland or Northern Ireland?
Scotland: same formula, same cap (employment law is reserved). Northern Ireland: governed by the Employment Rights (NI) Order 1996, which mirrors ERA 1996 in structure but sets its own week's pay cap. For 2026/27 the NI cap is £783 a week (max £23,490), higher than the £751 / £22,530 in Great Britain. Tribunal claims go to the Industrial Tribunal (NI), not the GB ET.
What if I am an agency worker?
Most agency workers are not employees and do not qualify for statutory redundancy. Some larger agencies employ workers on PAYE employment contracts; with 2 years' continuous employment WITH THE AGENCY, redundancy can be owed by the agency itself if no further work is found.
Do bank holidays count as weekly pay for the cap?
Weekly pay is your gross weekly remuneration for normal hours of work, including basic plus regular contractual elements. Bank holiday treatment depends on the contract; if you are normally paid for bank holidays, they form part of the weekly figure. The cap then applies.
What if I refuse a 4-week trial in the new role?
Refusing to enter the trial at all may be unreasonable, depending on the role offered. If you accept and the trial fails, your original redundancy entitlement is preserved (ERA 1996 s.138). If you refuse the trial outright without good reason, statutory redundancy can be lost.
