The redundancy cap from April 2027

The 2027/28 cap is not set yet: it is fixed by an Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order, which takes effect on 6 April. The formula it must follow is already known. Section 34 of the Employment Relations Act 1999 moves the cap by the September Retail Prices Index, rounded to the nearest whole pound (50p rounds up). The ONS September 2026 RPI was 3.9%.

April 2027 on the s.34 formula (not yet in force)
£751 × 1.039 = £780.29 → £780 a week
Maximum statutory payment on that cap: 20 yrs × 1.5 wk × £780 = £23,400

What the cap is, in one sentence

From 6 April 2026, statutory redundancy is calculated using a weekly pay figure no greater than £751 (ERA 1996 s.227↗). If you earn £1,200 a week gross, the calc still uses £751.

Why the cap exists

The cap is a deliberate Parliamentary choice to keep the statutory minimum predictable and to limit employer liability. It is not a definition of fair compensation. Many employers top up with enhanced schemes precisely because the statutory cap is below the typical UK median wage.

When the cap changes

The Secretary of State must review the cap each year and lay an Order under section 34 of the Employment Relations Act 1999. New rates take effect from 6 April. The September Retail Prices Index (RPI) figure drives the uprating (RPI is the index named in the statute, not CPI). If the September RPI is lower than the previous September's, section 34(2) requires the limits to fall by the same percentage, rounded to the nearest whole pound. Every Order since 2014 has been an increase.

For 2026/27 the cap rose from £719 to £751, a 4.5% increase, made by the Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order 2026 (SI 2026/310↗). The full Order can be cross-checked against the GOV.UK statutory limits collection.

Other places the cap applies

The same s.227 cap applies to the basic and additional awards in unfair dismissal claims. It does NOT apply to the protective award for failure to consult collectively under TULRCA 1992 s.189(4)↗ (s.227 does not list it, so a week's pay there is uncapped), nor to statutory notice pay (a week's pay for notice is uncapped; you get your actual weekly gross), nor to enhanced schemes that use uncapped pay.
