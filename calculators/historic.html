Which rate applies to your claim

The weekly cap is the one in force on the effective date of dismissal under ERA 1996 s.227↗. If your last day was 4 April 2026, the 2025/26 cap of £719 applies. If it was 6 April 2026 or later, you get £751. The uprating Order takes effect from 6 April each year.

Pre-1983 service

Service before 6 April 1983 has limits set by ERA 1996 s.211↗. The general rule: service before age 18 does not count, and the count starts from your 18th birthday or your start date, whichever is later. See the pre-1983 service rule.
