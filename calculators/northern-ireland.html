Statutory redundancy calculator: Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland is the only UK nation with its own statute. The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 mirrors ERA 1996 in structure, but sets its own week's pay cap: for 2026/27 it is £783 a week (higher than the £751 GB figure), giving a maximum statutory payment of £23,490.

Statutory basis

The governing instrument is the ERO (NI) 1996↗. It uses the same 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 weeks-per-year age-band structure as ERA 1996 s.162, but the week's pay cap is set separately by the Department for the Economy. For 2026/27 the NI cap is £783 a week (uprated from £749 by 4.5% under the Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order (NI) 2026), giving a maximum statutory payment of £23,490. That is higher than the £751 / £22,530 that applies in Great Britain.

The official NI calculator and guidance live at nidirect.gov.uk. Tribunal claims are heard by the Industrial Tribunal (NI), not the GB Employment Tribunal.

The redundancy pay formula is the same in both. The cap, the consultation periods and the bodies you deal with are not. Each row names the provision it comes from.

Northern Ireland redundancy cap by year

Redundancy pay is worked out on the cap in force on your relevant date (usually the day your notice ends), not the date you were told. Each April's figure follows the September RPI under Article 33 of the Employment Relations (NI) Order 1999. For the Great Britain figures see the GB rate history.

If your employer is insolvent in NI

In Great Britain you claim from the National Insurance Fund via the Insolvency Service. In Northern Ireland the Department for the Economy pays from the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund (ERO (NI) 1996 Art. 202↗), through its Redundancy Payments Service. Since May 2026 claims are made on its online portal: paper RP1 forms are no longer accepted for new cases, and you receive an email invitation to the portal once the insolvency practitioner has set up your case. The weekly cap on payments from the fund is the NI statutory figure of £783.

Questions about NI redundancy pay

How much is statutory redundancy pay in Northern Ireland?

For redundancies on or after 6 April 2026, Northern Ireland pays half a week's pay for each full year of service under age 22, one week's pay for each year aged 22 to 40 and one and a half weeks' pay for each year aged 41 or over, counting at most 20 years (ERO (NI) 1996 Art. 197). A week's pay is capped at £783 (Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order (NI) 2026), so the most anyone can get is £23,490.

Is redundancy pay higher in Northern Ireland than in England?

Yes, for anyone whose weekly pay is above the Great Britain cap. In 2026/27 the NI week's pay cap is £783 against £751 in England, Scotland and Wales, so the NI maximum is £23,490 against £22,530. If you earn £751 a week or less, both give the same result.

How long do you need to work for redundancy pay in Northern Ireland?

At least two years of continuous employment with your employer, the same as in Great Britain (nidirect). You must claim within six months of your job ending: the payment is agreed and paid, you claim it in writing from your employer, or you refer it to an industrial tribunal (ERO (NI) 1996 Art. 199).

How long is redundancy consultation in Northern Ireland?

Where an employer proposes 20 or more redundancies at one establishment within 90 days, consultation must start at least 30 days before the first dismissal, or at least 90 days before where 100 or more are proposed (ERO (NI) 1996 Art. 216). Great Britain requires 45 days for 100 or more. The NI protective award for failing to consult is capped at 90 days' pay (Art. 217).
